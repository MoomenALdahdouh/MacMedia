MacMedia — first launch on macOS
================================

1. Drag MacMedia into Applications.
2. Open the Applications folder.
3. Right-click MacMedia and choose Open.
4. Click Open in the dialog.

macOS may warn that the app is from an unidentified developer. That happens
because this build is not Apple-notarized. Right-click → Open is the normal
way to allow it on your Mac.

If it is still blocked, open Terminal and run:

  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/MacMedia.app

Then right-click → Open again.
